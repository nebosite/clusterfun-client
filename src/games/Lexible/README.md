# Lexible

A two-team word game. The shared screen shows a grid of letters; everyone plays from their
phone at the same time.

**How to play.** Drag across neighbouring letters to spell a word. A valid word claims every
tile you used for your team — and a claimed tile can only be taken by a **longer** word than
the one that won it. Letters connect in any direction, including diagonally. Tap a letter to
see words you could build from it.

**How to win.** Own an unbroken path of tiles from your team's side of the board to the far
side. Paths connect up, down, left and right only — not diagonally.
