# RetroSpectro

A team **retrospective**, run as a ClusterFun game. Unlike the party games here it is a work
tool — usually shown on a meeting-room screen or over a screen-share, with everyone
contributing from their phone.

**How it runs.**

1. **Brainstorm** — everyone submits ideas from their phone, marked 👍 went-well or
   👎 needs-work. Ideas arrive anonymously and stay hidden until sorting.
2. **Categorize** — the facilitator drags related ideas together on the big screen. Groups
   name themselves from the words their ideas share, and **+ / −** weights a card so the
   things people care about sort to the top.
3. **Talk** — one group at a time, biggest first, with Notes and Tasks captured per group.
   **Show summary** produces a plain-text export of the whole retro.
